Built for three people

One platform, three views of the same gift.

The platform stays quiet so each merchant's card artwork can be the colour. Every screen works on desktop and on the phone.

Platform admin

See every client, card and ringgit in circulation from one console.

  • Revenue, card sales and client health at a glance
  • Users across every role with suspend and invite
  • Platform fee, card validity and notification templates

Merchant

Design branded cards, show a payment QR in one tap and connect your own gateway.

  • Static or amount QR with a five-minute timer
  • Card studio for Fixed, Open Value and Voucher cards
  • DuitNow QR, Billplz, ToyyibPay, Stripe or bank transfer

Customer

A phone wallet for buying, gifting and spending cards from every merchant.

  • Tap a card to flip to its balance and QR
  • Send a card with a message and a gold ribbon
  • Scan the counter QR and pay, top up or redeem
How it works

From a merchant's artwork to a tap at the counter.

1

Merchant designs a card

Upload artwork, pick the card type, set the value and publish to the catalogue.

2

Customer buys it

Choose a design and amount, then pay through the merchant's own gateway.

3

Gift it to someone

Send by phone number with a message. The recipient accepts it into their wallet.

4

Pay by QR

Scan the counter QR. The card type decides the flow and the platform updates the balance.

Card types

One card, one type, a flow that always fits.

Tap a card to flip it. The type is fixed when the card is created and printed on its face.
